Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Grow's Privacy Policy. At Grow ("we", "us", or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

Last updated: November 26, 2025

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register for a Grow account or use our services, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password
  • Profile Information: Username, profile picture, and preferences
  • Payment Information: Billing details, payment method (processed securely through Stripe)
  • Contact Information: Email address for communications and support

Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you interact with our platform:

  • Content Creation Data: Prompts, media uploaded, content created, and generation settings
  • Platform Usage: Features used, time spent, pages visited, and navigation patterns
  • Performance Data: Content generation success rates, loading times, and error logs
  • Credit Usage: Credit balance, transactions, and subscription status

Device and Technical Information

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Connection Data: Internet service provider, referring/exit pages, and clickstream data
  • Cookies and Tracking: Information collected through cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy)

User-Generated Content

  • Text Prompts: Text descriptions you provide for content generation
  • Uploaded Media: Audio files, video files, and images you upload to our platform
  • Generated Content: Content created using our platform
  • Feedback and Support: Messages, bug reports, and feature requests

Information About Children

Important Notice: Grow is designed for use by adults (individuals 18 years of age or older). We do not direct our services to children and do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area).

However, our platform allows adult users to upload media (audio, video, images) and provide information about children (such as name, birthday, and gender) for the purpose of creating personalized content. When you provide such information:

  • You represent and warrant that you are the parent or legal guardian of any child whose information you provide
  • You consent to our collection and use of this information as described in this Privacy Policy
  • You understand that you are responsible for obtaining any necessary consents for uploading media containing children
  • Such information is processed solely to provide our services to you (the adult user) and is subject to the same protections as other user data

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us without parental consent, please contact us immediately at support@memorylabai.com, and we will delete such information promptly.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

Service Delivery

  • Provide and maintain the Grow platform
  • Process your content generation requests
  • Manage your account, credits, and subscription
  • Process payments and prevent fraud
  • Store and deliver your generated content

Communication

  • Send transactional emails (account verification, password resets, receipts)
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries
  • Notify you about changes to our services or policies
  • Send important service announcements and updates

Improvement and Analytics

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our platform performance
  • Monitor and analyze trends to enhance user experience
  • Develop new features and services
  • Conduct research and development for better content quality

Security and Compliance

  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and abuse
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and protect user safety
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements
  • Protect our rights, privacy, safety, and property

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

We work with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:

  • Payment Processing: Stripe for secure payment transactions
  • Cloud Storage: AWS S3 for storing generated content and uploaded media
  • Email Services: Resend for transactional emails and notifications
  • Authentication: Google OAuth for social login functionality
  • AI Services: Third-party AI providers for content generation capabilities

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to:

  • Legal processes (subpoenas, court orders, or legal requirements)
  • Requests from government authorities or law enforcement
  • Protection of our rights, property, or safety
  • Investigation of potential violations of our Terms of Service

Business Transfers

If Grow is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

With Your Consent

We may share your information for any other purpose with your explicit consent.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • Secure Infrastructure: Industry-standard cloud infrastructure with regular security updates
  • Payment Security: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing through Stripe
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security assessments and vulnerability testing

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy:

  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active, plus 90 days after account deletion
  • Generated Content: Stored according to your plan and deleted after the retention period
  • Usage Data: Anonymized and aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for analytics
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years to comply with tax and financial regulations

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support@memorylabai.com.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

Access and Portability

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Request information about how we process your data

Correction and Deletion

  • Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (right to be forgotten)
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances

Consent and Objection

  • Withdraw consent for processing based on consent
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Opt-out of marketing communications (while still receiving essential service emails)

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@memorylabai.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

We ensure that international transfers comply with applicable data protection laws through:
